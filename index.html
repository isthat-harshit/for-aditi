A letter I wanted you to have

For my Love, Aditi

Dear Love,

I don't think I'm very good at putting feelings into words. Most of the time, I joke around, act stupid, or keep things to myself. So I thought maybe making you this little corner of the internet would be easier than trying to say everything perfectly.

I love the little things about you just as much as the big ones. Your smile, your eyes, your hair, your personality — and yes, your boyfriend too. 😂 But somewhere along the way, it stopped being about the things I like about you. It became about you.

I still think about the moment when I saw you crying because of what was happening with your family. I don't usually let other people's pain reach me very deeply. But seeing you hurt did something to me. It made me realise that your happiness matters to me in a way I can't really explain.

And then there are the completely ridiculous moments — like us fighting in front of Central Park over whose shoes were getting dirty, as if we were actually Oggy and the Cockroaches. Those are the moments I hope we never lose. The silly ones. The ordinary ones. The ones that don't look important until you realise later that they're the memories you miss the most.

I don't know exactly what every tomorrow will look like. But I know I want more of those tomorrows with you. More laughs. More stupid arguments. More pictures. More late conversations. More memories that only make sense to us.

So whenever life gets heavy, whenever you doubt yourself, or whenever you simply forget how special you are, come back here and remember one thing: there is someone who sees you, chooses you, and loves having you in his life.

You are my favourite part of this story. ❤️

Always,
Harshit
